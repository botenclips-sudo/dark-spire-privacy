Dark Spire: Last Stand privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. Published by LowTorch games. Contact: darkspire.support@gmail.com

Dark Spire: Last Stand ("the game") is a single-player game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play and why.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress, settings and rune balance are saved on your own device. They are not sent to us, and we do not run any servers that collect information about you. The only gameplay information that leaves your device is the optional statistics described below. Deleting the game deletes this data from your device. The Save backup screen can show a code that holds your progress; it stays on your device unless you copy it somewhere yourself.

If your phone backs up app data to your own iCloud or Google account, your progress may be included in that backup. Those backups belong to you and are handled by Apple or Google; we cannot see them.

Tournaments and warbands

Tournaments, leaderboards and warbands run entirely on your device against computer-controlled players. The name you choose, your level and your scores are not sent anywhere.

Reminders

If you choose to turn on reminders, the game schedules notifications on your own device, for example when your vigil is full or your daily blessing is ready. They are created and delivered by your device's operating system. No information is sent to us or to anyone else to do this. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Gameplay statistics

To balance the game and see where new players get stuck, the app can send gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: when a run ends (the realm, the wave reached, how long it lasted and the runes earned), level-ups, first milestones such as the first upgrade or the first boss defeated, realms opened, rewarded ads watched and which items were bought (never prices or payment details). They are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email or advertising identifier. Google also receives your IP address and the approximate location it reveals, and device information such as the model, operating system and language.

You can turn this off at any time in the game's Settings under "Share gameplay stats". In regions where Google's consent form applies (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), statistics stay off until you turn them on. We keep these statistics for no more than 14 months. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location it reveals, information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

On iPhone, the game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you are in a region that requires consent, Google's consent form lets you choose how your data is used, and you can reopen it from the game's Settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (or Google Play on Android). We never see your payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Emails you send us

If you email us, for support, to report an ad or to ask about your data, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as we need to.

Retention and security

We keep no player data on servers of our own. Google (for ads and gameplay statistics) and RevenueCat keep the data described above under their own policies and only as long as they need it for those purposes. Connections to them are encrypted.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking for the game in your device settings.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Buy the Warden's Seal (formerly Remove Ads) to skip ads: their rewards are given without an ad. The ads service still starts with the game, so the tracking and consent choices above still apply.
  • Turn reminders on or off in the game's Settings.
  • Turn gameplay statistics on or off in the game's Settings.
  • Delete your data by deleting the game. To ask about data held by our partners (ads and purchases), contact us and we will point you to them.
  • Contact us at darkspire.support@gmail.com with any question or request about your data.

Delete your data

Dark Spire: Last Stand by LowTorch games has no accounts, and your progress is stored only on your device. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its app data in your device settings. If your phone backs up app data to your iCloud or Google account, delete the game's backup there too.

To ask for data held by our partners to be deleted (Google AdMob for ads, Google Analytics for Firebase for gameplay statistics, RevenueCat for purchases):

  1. Email darkspire.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. If you made purchases, include your Google Play or App Store order number so we can find your purchase record.
  3. We reply within 30 days. We ask RevenueCat to delete your purchase history and anonymous app user ID, ask Google to delete the gameplay statistics linked to your device, and point you to Google's tools for advertising data. Records that app stores or tax law require us to keep may be kept for as long as that law requires.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
